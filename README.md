# Factory360 Suggestions

A one-page website that summarizes our Strategic HR recommendations for **Factory360**, prepared for **Gabriela Neves**, President of Factory360.

Open `index.html` in any browser. You can also publish it with GitHub Pages: go to **Settings → Pages → Deploy from a branch**, pick the branch and `/ (root)`, and save.

**Sections:** Summary · What we heard · People & staffing · AI & technology · Feedback & performance · Benefits & rewards · Legal & compliance · Budget & outsourcing · Culture & competitive edge · 12-month roadmap
